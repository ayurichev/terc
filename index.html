Pass the Czech gun-permit exam.

Terč is the fastest, cleanest way to prepare for the theoretical part of the Czech gun-permit exam (zbrojní průkaz) — and it works entirely offline. No accounts, no sign-up, no network. Just you and the questions.

Complete question bank

Every question from the official set, organized by category, with correct answers highlighted and jump-to-number search.

Learning mode that sticks

Pick how many questions and a cadence; get reminded by notification or a calendar slot. Anything you miss comes back until you get it right.

Realistic exam simulation

A timed 30-question mock that mirrors the structure and pass thresholds of the real test.

Obecné & Rozšířené scope

Switch between the general and extended permit scopes so your practice matches the permit you're after.

Built for everyone

Full Dynamic Type, rotation, and zoomable images. Interface in Czech, English, Russian, and Ukrainian.

Privacy by design

Collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no network requests. Everything stays on your device.

By Czech law the exam is conducted in Czech only, so questions are always shown in Czech. Terč can display an unofficial translation of any question for convenience — clearly marked, and never a substitute for the Czech original.
