Terč — Czech gun-permit (zbrojní průkaz) exam prep

Terč helps you prepare for the theoretical part of the Czech gun-permit exam. It works entirely offline and needs no account. Here’s how to get the most out of it, and answers to common questions.

Getting started

  • Questions — browse the full official question bank by category. Correct answers are highlighted; tap the number to jump straight to any question.
  • Learning — choose how many questions to study and how often (daily, every few days, or weekly) at a time you pick. Terč reminds you with a notification, or adds a study slot to your calendar. Each session has you read, then answer; anything you miss comes back until you get it right.
  • Exam — take a timed 30-question simulation that mirrors the structure and pass thresholds of the real test.
  • Settings — switch between the Obecné (general) and Rozšířené (extended) permit scopes, choose the interface language, and manage reminders.

Frequently asked questions

My learning reminders aren’t appearing.
Reminders are local notifications. Make sure notifications are allowed for Terč in iOS Settings → Notifications → Terč, and that Focus / Do Not Disturb isn’t hiding them. Prefer not to use notifications? In Learning, choose the calendar option instead and Terč will add study events to your calendar.
Why are the questions only in Czech?
By Czech law the gun-permit exam is conducted in Czech only, so the questions are always shown in Czech. For convenience you can tap to see an unofficial translation of a question and its answers in your language — it’s clearly marked as unofficial and is never a substitute for the Czech original.
Is my data synced or backed up to the cloud?
No. Terč is fully offline. Your settings and progress are stored only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
How does the exam simulation work?
It selects questions in line with how the official exam is assembled and applies the real test’s 30-question format and pass thresholds, respecting your chosen permit scope (Obecné / Rozšířené).
I think an answer or translation is wrong.
The Czech question text follows the official question bank. If you spot a problem with a translation or a typo, please email us using the address below — include the question number if you can.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
