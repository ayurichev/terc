Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Terč (“the app”) is designed to be completely private and to work entirely offline. This policy explains what that means in practice. In short: the app collects no personal data, and nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

No data collection

Terč does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are:

  • no user accounts, sign-up, or login;
  • no analytics, usage tracking, or crash reporting;
  • no advertising and no advertising identifiers;
  • no third-party SDKs that collect data.

No network access

The app makes no network requests of any kind. It does not connect to the internet, to us, or to any third party. All questions, answers, and content are bundled inside the app and read locally.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences and progress — such as the permit scope (Obecné / Rozšířené), learning settings, reminder schedule, and study progress — are saved locally on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). This data stays on your device, is removed when you delete the app, and is only included in your own device backups if you have those enabled.

Notifications and calendar

If you choose to set up learning reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device, or — if you prefer — adds study events to your calendar. These are created locally on your device using the permissions you grant in iOS Settings. No reminder or calendar information is sent to us or anyone else. You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
